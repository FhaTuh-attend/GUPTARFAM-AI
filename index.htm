What can GuptarFam automate today?

Currently available: • Generate AI responses. • Draft content, emails and business plans. • Plan automation workflows. • Save your chat history in this browser. • Navigate dashboard sections using voice commands. Not connected yet: • Automatically publishing social media posts. • Sending emails from your account. • Executing trades or controlling MT5. • Running workflows while the browser is closed. Those features need additional integrations, permissions and testing.
